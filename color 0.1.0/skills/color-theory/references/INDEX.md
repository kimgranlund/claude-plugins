# Color Theory References

Curated transcripts, notes, and source material for the `color-theory` pack — harmony, the color
wheel and its history, colorists' programmes, and color meaning/mood. Extracted 2026-07-02 from
the `color-science` pack, whose remainder — perception science, contrast math, naming standards,
pigment physics — was itself split 2026-07-06 into the four `color-science-*` packs.

**28 files**, organized by ask-axis below. Subdirs preserve the original taxonomy: `historical/`
(12 — wheels, theorists, colorists), `contemporary/` (5 — research & programmes), `techniques/`
(11 — applied harmony & practice).
