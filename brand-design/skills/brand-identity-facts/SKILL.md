# brand-identity-facts — the identity critic's lens, grounded in the council roster

What the identity-design family of `check-brand-council` critics KNOWS and JUDGES about a visual
identity system — the questions they ask, the defects they catch, the concrete tests they run,
and the vocabulary they share. This is `make-critic`'s grounding corpus for minting a new
identity-family critic; it is not a procedure and does not itself run a review.

4 declared axes (pack-writing-rules' 3-7 threshold) across 4 reference files, flat corpus — this
table is the retrieval map (no INDEX needed at this size).

Enter by search: Grep the mapped file for the term, then Read that section — never read the
folder start-to-finish. Every consult answers as claim + the cited reference file + its
grounding marker ([verified] against a persona file, or [inferred] and marked as such) — e.g.
"a mark that shatters at 16px fails Paula S.'s liquidity stress test ([verified],
`references/canonical-tests.md`)."

## The clustering evidence (why these four sit together)

`critic-jessica-w` (find the weird / human signature / brand therapy), `critic-massimo-v`
(discipline, timelessness, three-level coherence), `critic-matt-w` (editorial typography as the
lead element), and `critic-paula-s` (liquid identity, scale, editorial refusal) all judge the
VISUAL/SYSTEMIC IDENTITY artifact — marks, type, grid, color, behavior across contexts and years
— never the strategic substance behind it or the campaign/copy layer built on top of it. Full
per-critic evidence lines: `references/lenses.md`.

## Fences

- **Against the sibling role-family packs** (`brand-strategy-facts`, `brand-voice-facts`,
  `brand-advertising-facts`) — each pack owns a disjoint set of council critics and a disjoint
  lens. A question about positioning substance, copy craft, or a campaign's Big Idea routes to
  the owning sibling, never answered here.
- **Against `council-rules`** — that pack is the domain-neutral council MACHINERY; this pack is
  ROLE KNOWLEDGE for one family of critics inside that machinery. Neither restates the other.
- **Against `brand-methodology-rules`** — that pack is the MAKER's method for building the
  identity/expression system in the first place; this pack is the JUDGE's lens on the result. A
  "how do I design an expression system" question routes to `brand-methodology-rules`; a "would
  the council accept this identity system" question routes here.
- **Against `make-critic`** — that skill is the MINTING PROCEDURE; this pack is the GROUNDING
  CORPUS it reads before drafting or overlap-checking a new identity-family persona. Neither
  restates the other.
