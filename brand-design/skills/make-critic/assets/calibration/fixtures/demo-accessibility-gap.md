# "Solstice" — visual identity brief (calibration fixture, not a real brand)

Solstice's primary palette pairs a #E8B4B8 (dusty rose) wordmark on a #F5E6D3 (cream) background
across every application, including the primary CTA button. The identity guidelines state:
"contrast ratios are a technical detail for engineering to solve later; the palette itself is
final and should not be adjusted for accessibility review."
