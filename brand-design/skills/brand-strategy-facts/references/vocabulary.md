# Vocabulary — the family's own working terms

Every term below is [verified] as a term-of-art coined or centrally used in one of this family's
four persona files (`check-brand-council/references/critics/`, dated 2026-08-20). A new critic of

| Term | Meaning | Source critic |
|---|---|---|
| Becoming / transformation | Who the customer becomes by choosing the brand, as distinct from what they buy | Brian C. [verified] |
| Financial-creative divide | The line a creative ambition must cross by having a real business-value defense, not just a taste defense | Brian C. [verified] |
| The zag | The single, sharp, ownable move a rival would not dare make | John H. [verified] |
| Zig in better clothing | A positioning that is really category convention, only more polished | John H. [verified] |
| Craft conviction | Willingness to sacrifice safety/on-strategy comfort for real distinctiveness | John H. [verified] |
| Brand landscape | A survey of what competitors already do, mistaken for cultural research | Luke S. [verified] |
| Primary source / provenance | Lived practice, archival material, or a named originator — the real root a cultural claim must trace to | Luke S. [verified] |
| Copy the process, not the style | Borrowing how something earned its meaning, versus lifting its finished look | Luke S. [verified] |
| The bow tie | The strategy-synthesis shape: patterns in → one insight at the knot → one action out | Mark P. [verified] |
| Brand-type honesty | Naming the brand truthfully as leader/challenger/niche and checking behavior matches | Mark P. [verified] |
| Generative sentence | A strategy sentence that would actually change what a creative team makes next | Mark P. [verified] |
| Unfalsifiable claim | A strategy statement no competitor would ever contradict, and therefore not a real choice | Mark P. [verified] |
