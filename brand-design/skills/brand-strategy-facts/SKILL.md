# brand-strategy-facts — the strategy critic's lens, grounded in the council roster

What the strategy family of `check-brand-council` critics KNOWS and JUDGES — the questions they
ask, the defects they catch, the concrete tests they run, and the vocabulary they share. This is
`make-critic`'s grounding corpus for minting a new strategy-family critic; it is not a procedure
and does not itself run a review.

4 declared axes (pack-writing-rules' 3-7 threshold) across 4 reference files, flat corpus — this
table is the retrieval map (no INDEX needed at this size).

Enter by search: Grep the mapped file for the term, then Read that section — never read the
folder start-to-finish. Every consult answers as claim + the cited reference file + its
grounding marker ([verified] against a persona file, or [inferred] and marked as such) — e.g.
"a positioning that a rival could also claim fails John H.'s zag test ([verified],
`references/canonical-tests.md`)."

## The clustering evidence (why these four sit together)

`critic-brian-c` (transformation & financial-creative fluency), `critic-john-h`
(differentiation/the zag), `critic-luke-s` (cultural provenance & research depth), and
`critic-mark-p` (language discipline & the strategy bow tie) all judge the STRATEGIC ARTIFACT
itself — a positioning call, a Foundation Canon, a brand-type claim — against a substance-over-
assembly test, never the expression layer (typography, campaign execution, copy craft). Full
per-critic evidence lines: `references/lenses.md`.

## Fences

- **Against the sibling role-family packs** (`brand-identity-facts`, `brand-voice-facts`,
  `brand-advertising-facts`) — each pack owns a disjoint set of council critics and a disjoint
  lens (strategic substance vs. visual identity vs. copy craft vs. campaign/creative execution).
  A strategy question wandering into typography, house style, or a campaign's big idea routes to
  the owning sibling, never answered here.
- **Against `council-rules`** — that pack is the domain-neutral council MACHINERY (fan-out,
  severity, voting, synthesis, the two-phase model); this pack is ROLE KNOWLEDGE for one family of
  critics inside that machinery. This pack never restates fan-out mechanics or the severity
  taxonomy; `council-rules` never restates a strategy-family lens.
- **Against `brand-methodology-rules`** — that pack is the MAKER's method (how to build a
  Foundation Canon, a positioning territory, a transformation story); this pack is the JUDGE's
  lens on that same territory (what a critic checks the maker's output against). The maker builds
  toward the Foundation Canon; the critic tests whether the result actually clears the becoming/
  zag/provenance/bow-tie bars. Neither pack restates the other's content — a maker-side "how do I
  write a positioning territory" question routes to `brand-methodology-rules`; a judge-side "would
  the council accept this positioning" question routes here.
- **Against `make-critic`** — that skill is the MINTING PROCEDURE (template, `.name-map.md`
  discipline, roster registration, calibration fixture, checker pass); this pack is the GROUNDING
  CORPUS `make-critic` reads before drafting a new strategy-family persona, and before checking a
  proposed lens for overlap against `failure-modes.md`/`canonical-tests.md`. This pack never
  restates the minting steps; `make-critic` never restates a strategy-family lens itself — it
  cites this pack instead of re-deriving from the 14 raw persona files alone.
