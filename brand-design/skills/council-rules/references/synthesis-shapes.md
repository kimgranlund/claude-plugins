# The five synthesis shapes

Synthesis is the most important step a council runs — the individual critiques are inputs to it,
not the deliverable. Every council instance resolves its collected findings through the same five
prompts, in order, regardless of domain. A domain instance may letter/number them for its own
citation convention (brand's are `B-S1`–`B-S5`); the five shapes themselves are the machinery.

## 1 — Convergence

Which failure did **two or more** critics independently name, from their own distinct lenses,
without having seen each other's read (this is why the blind phase must stay genuinely blind —
`two-phase-model.md`)? Convergence is the highest-confidence problem a council can report — lead
with it.

## 2 — Highest severity

Across all critics, the single most load-bearing finding: the one that, unaddressed, makes the
rest of the findings moot. Name it and why it outranks everything else raised, even a Critical
finding from a different critic that doesn't share this property.

## 3 — Productive tension

Where do two critics genuinely disagree — not on severity (that's `severity-and-voting.md`'s
2-of-3 job), but on STANCE: one critic's lens demands a choice the other's lens rejects. The
disagreement is information, not noise to average away: what does it reveal about the work's real,
unresolved choice? A synthesis that resolves every tension into a single verdict has thrown away
the most interesting thing a council can produce.

## 4 — The blind spot

What would ALL the selected critics miss, structurally, given the sub-council(s) actually
convened? A strategy council will not catch a typographic failure; a design council will not catch
a hollow positioning. Name the gap explicitly and recommend the sub-council that would catch it —
this is the sub-council mechanism's own escape hatch (`roster-and-personas.md`), and skipping this
prompt is how a scoped review quietly gets read as a complete one.

## 5 — Verdict + the top revisions

Does the work meet the standard the council was convened to hold it to? State the verdict plainly,
then the top few revisions that would most raise the work — each attributed to the critic(s) whose
lens demands it, never presented as the synthesis's own unattributed opinion. A domain instance
fixes its own count (brand's is three); the count is configuration, the attribution requirement is
not.

## What synthesis is not

Synthesis never re-scores a finding a critic already scored (that's what 2-of-3 voting is for on
a genuinely contested one), never invents a finding no critic raised, and never suppresses a
finding that survived the trust-boundary check just because it's inconvenient for a clean verdict.
