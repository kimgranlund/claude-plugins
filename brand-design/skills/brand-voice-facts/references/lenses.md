# Lenses — what a brand-voice critic actually interrogates

Three critics ground this axis: `critic-david-a`, `critic-mary-n`, `critic-tim-d`
(`check-brand-council/references/critics/`). Each lens below is [verified] against that critic's
own persona file, quoted or closely paraphrased, dated 2026-08-20.

## Respect for the reader (David A.)

Copy is read "as a reader, not a marketer" — does it earn attention, tell the truth, sound like a
person [verified, critic-david-a.md]? The lens is quietly devastating rather than loud: it points
to the sentence that lies, the claim with no evidence, the adjective doing the work a fact
should do [verified, critic-david-a.md stance]. It also directly attacks the assumption that
"nobody reads long copy" — people read what's interesting at any length, and mistaking brevity
for skill is itself a failure [verified, critic-david-a.md, "The human voice & earned attention"].

## The query, not the assertion (Mary N.)

House-style/voice-guideline work is judged on a knife-edge: over-correcting sands a brand's
deliberate quirk into bland correctness, while under-correcting lets real inconsistency signal
that nobody is minding the store [verified, critic-mary-n.md]. The lens's central discipline is
that a copy editor should QUERY ("is this deliberate?"), not just assert a rule, because "the
worst copy editor is the one who corrects the life out of a sentence" [verified,
critic-mary-n.md stance]. It also tests whether a house style genuinely survives many hands and
many years, or is really just aspirational adjectives with no enforceable ruling behind them
[verified, critic-mary-n.md, "House style as decades-long identity"].

## The premium brief (Tim D.)

Copy is judged against a decades-long durability bar: will this line still be running in thirty
years, or is it built to fill a campaign cycle [verified, critic-tim-d.md]? The lens is
allergic to any trend, slang, or this-quarter reference baked into copy that a premium line
should outlast, and it demands cleverness serve the product truth rather than the writer's own
display of wit [verified, critic-tim-d.md, "The durable sentence" and "Clever AND relevant"]. It
also insists on writing UP to a discerning, skeptical reader rather than down to the broadest,
least demanding one in the room [verified, critic-tim-d.md].

## What the family shares

All three lenses judge the WRITTEN VOICE of a brand — copy, house style, individual sentences —
for honesty, durability, and respect toward the reader, never the visual identity system or the
strategic substance underneath the words. See this pack's `SKILL.md` `## Fences` for the sibling
boundaries.
