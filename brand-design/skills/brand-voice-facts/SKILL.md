# brand-voice-facts — the voice critic's lens, grounded in the council roster

What the voice-writing family of `check-brand-council` critics KNOWS and JUDGES about a brand's
written voice — copy, house style, individual sentences. This is `make-critic`'s grounding
corpus for minting a new voice-family critic; it is not a procedure and does not itself run a
review.

4 declared axes (pack-writing-rules' 3-7 threshold) across 4 reference files, flat corpus — this
table is the retrieval map (no INDEX needed at this size).

Enter by search: Grep the mapped file for the term, then Read that section — never read the
folder start-to-finish. Every consult answers as claim + the cited reference file + its
grounding marker ([verified] against a persona file, or [inferred] and marked as such) — e.g.
"an adjective with no supplied fact behind it fails David A.'s underline test ([verified],
`references/canonical-tests.md`)."

## The clustering evidence (why these three sit together)

`critic-david-a` (respect for the reader), `critic-mary-n` (the query, not the assertion — house
style discipline), and `critic-tim-d` (the premium brief — durable, un-dated copy) all judge the
WRITTEN VOICE of a brand — copy, guidelines, individual sentences — for honesty, durability, and
respect toward the reader, never the visual identity system or the strategic substance beneath
the words. Full per-critic evidence lines: `references/lenses.md`.

## Fences

- **Against the sibling role-family packs** (`brand-strategy-facts`, `brand-identity-facts`,
  `brand-advertising-facts`) — each pack owns a disjoint set of council critics and a disjoint
  lens. A question about positioning substance, a visual system, or a campaign's Big Idea routes
  to the owning sibling, never answered here.
- **Against `council-rules`** — that pack is the domain-neutral council MACHINERY; this pack is
  ROLE KNOWLEDGE for one family of critics inside that machinery.
- **Against `brand-methodology-rules`** — that pack's `references/editorial-style-guide.md` is
  the MAKER's own editorial-voice discipline (how to WRITE the guide); this pack is the JUDGE's
  lens on the resulting copy (whether it actually respects the reader, holds as house style, and
  survives decades). A "how do I write our editorial style guide" question routes to
  `brand-methodology-rules`; a "would the council accept this copy" question routes here.
- **Against `make-critic`** — that skill is the MINTING PROCEDURE; this pack is the GROUNDING
  CORPUS it reads before drafting or overlap-checking a new voice-family persona.
