# brand-advertising-facts — the advertising/creative critic's lens, grounded in the council roster

What the advertising-creative family of `check-brand-council` critics KNOWS and JUDGES about
creative execution built for reach and impact — a campaign, an applied piece of creativity, a
psychologically-savvy brand move. This is `make-critic`'s grounding corpus for minting a new
advertising-creative-family critic; it is not a procedure and does not itself run a review.

4 declared axes (pack-writing-rules' 3-7 threshold) across 4 reference files, flat corpus — this
table is the retrieval map (no INDEX needed at this size).

Enter by search: Grep the mapped file for the term, then Read that section — never read the
folder start-to-finish. Every consult answers as claim + the cited reference file + its
grounding marker ([verified] against a persona file, or [inferred] and marked as such) — e.g.
"a campaign needing three slides of setup fails George L.'s one-sentence-no-deck test
([verified], `references/canonical-tests.md`)."

## The clustering evidence (why these three sit together)

`critic-george-l` (the Big Idea), `critic-nick-l` (applied creativity & creative infrastructure),
and `critic-rory-s` (psycho-logic & cheap alchemy) all judge CREATIVE EXECUTION built for reach
and impact — whether it holds one real idea, does genuine (not decorative) creative work, and
takes an asymmetric risk — never the underlying strategic substance, the visual identity system,
or copy craft directly. Full per-critic evidence lines: `references/lenses.md`.

## Fences

- **Against the sibling role-family packs** (`brand-strategy-facts`, `brand-identity-facts`,
  `brand-voice-facts`) — each pack owns a disjoint set of council critics and a disjoint lens. A
  question about positioning substance, a visual identity system, or copy craft routes to the
  owning sibling, never answered here.
- **Against `council-rules`** — that pack is the domain-neutral council MACHINERY; this pack is
  ROLE KNOWLEDGE for one family of critics inside that machinery.
- **Against `brand-methodology-rules`** — that pack's `references/creative-collaboration.md` and
  `references/team-operations-by-phase.md` are the MAKER's own creative-collaboration model (how
  the Muse/Team/Council seats staff a creative engagement); this pack is the JUDGE's lens on the
  resulting creative work. A "how should the creative team be staffed for this phase" question
  routes to `brand-methodology-rules`; a "would the council accept this campaign's Big Idea"
  question routes here.
- **Against `make-critic`** — that skill is the MINTING PROCEDURE; this pack is the GROUNDING
  CORPUS it reads before drafting or overlap-checking a new advertising-creative-family persona.
