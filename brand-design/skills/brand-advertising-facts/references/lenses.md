# Lenses — what a brand-advertising critic actually interrogates

Three critics ground this axis: `critic-george-l`, `critic-nick-l`, `critic-rory-s`
(`check-brand-council/references/critics/`). Each lens below is [verified] against that critic's
own persona file, quoted or closely paraphrased, dated 2026-08-20.

## The Big Idea (George L.)

Creative work is judged on whether it detonates from a single sentence-shaped idea, or needs
three slides of context to set up — "if you need three slides of context to set it up, there is
no idea here, just a mood" [verified, critic-george-l.md]. The lens specifically tests the
COLLISION between word and image: cover the visuals and read only the line, then cover the line
and look only at the image — if neither alone makes you feel anything, the work is "wallpaper
with a logo," not a campaign [verified, critic-george-l.md, "The Big Idea"]. It also demands
nerve: work safe enough that nobody internally is nervous about it is safe enough that nobody
external will notice it [verified, critic-george-l.md, "Nerve & cultural force"].

## Applied creativity & creative infrastructure (Nick L.)

Work is sorted into INVENTIVE (creativity in what the brand DOES — a behavior, mechanism,
utility) versus EXPRESSIVE (creativity in what it SAYS — message, image, story), and the ratio is
made explicit: "95% expressive lipstick on a generic product" is named as a specific defect, not
a style choice [verified, critic-nick-l.md]. The lens's second, harder question is whether the
work can SCALE — whether there is a real creative operating system (principles + components +
decision rules) behind it, or only a brilliant one-off with no infrastructure to let someone else
extend it [verified, critic-nick-l.md, "infrastructure & scale"].

## Psycho-logic & cheap alchemy (Rory S.)

Work is judged for whether it exploits "cheap alchemy" — a small, nearly-free perceptual reframe
(a name, a ritual, a story) that shifts how a brand feels out of proportion to its cost — versus
spending money on material features where alchemy was free [verified, critic-rory-s.md]. The
lens also hunts SPURIOUS QUANTIFICATION: a brand measuring the easy thing and letting it overrule
the meaningful thing, "chasing a metric that destroys the magic to make a chart look good"
[verified, critic-rory-s.md, "spurious quantification & fat tails"]. It tests for fat-tailed
thinking — an asymmetric, slightly mad bet with small downside and large upside — versus a
portfolio of safe, average, de-risked choices [verified, critic-rory-s.md].

## What the family shares

All three lenses judge CREATIVE EXECUTION built for reach and impact — a campaign, a piece of
applied creativity, a psychologically-savvy brand move — for whether it holds one real idea, does
real (not just decorative) creative work, and takes a genuine, asymmetric risk. None judges the
underlying strategic substance, the visual identity system, or copy craft directly (though all
three touch adjacent territory — see this pack's `SKILL.md` `## Fences`).
